# Ask before emailing

## When to use

Any time a step would send an email from Fitnito. This includes welcome emails, password resets, and family link requests.

## Steps

1. Look up the person. If more than one matches, ask which one.
2. Say exactly who gets the email and what kind it is. Then ask. For example: "Send a welcome email to Casey Nguyen (casey.nguyen@example.com)?"
3. Send it only after a clear yes in the reply to that question. A yes to something earlier doesn't count.
4. Confirm it was sent, and to which address.

## Keep in mind

- One confirmation covers one email to one person. For a group, list everyone first and get one yes for that exact list.
- If they say no or don't answer, don't send it. Offer to do the rest of the task without the email.
- Adding a member does not mean they want to email them. Ask separately.
